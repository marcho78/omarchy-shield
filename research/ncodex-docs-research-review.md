## Documentation conclusion

The research is unusually well traced into commits. The largest documentation debt is not missing outcomes, but stale earlier prose: the Plan’s `fastboot boot`, PMIC-cause heading, absent-fan statements, and pre-fix GPU/EMC conclusions should point to their later corrections.
